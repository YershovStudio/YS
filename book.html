Info & Policies

A few things to know before requesting a session.

How is my date reserved?+

Your date is reserved after I approve the session details and the $50 booking retainer is paid. The remaining balance is due according to the terms provided with your booking.

What happens if the weather is bad?+

For outdoor sessions, we can reschedule when weather would prevent us from creating the planned work. We’ll make that decision as the session approaches.

Can I bring multiple outfits?+

Yes. Outfit changes can be planned during the consultation so they support the concept and fit comfortably within the session.

How do I choose my final photos?+

You’ll receive a proof gallery after the shoot and select the images you want professionally edited and delivered.

Do you travel?+

Yes. Sessions outside the Lowell area may include an additional travel fee depending on distance and production needs.
