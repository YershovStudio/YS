The Process

01 |

Consultation

﹀

We begin with a discussion to define your project goals and create a moodboard to visualize ideas. We discuss the aesthetic direction, outfits, makeup, and location, to create the best possible results.

02 |

Photography

﹀

On the shoot day, we execute the plan in a studio or on-location. This phase is entirely focused on visual storytelling, using lighting, poses, and experimentation to capture your emotions within the environment.

03 |

Editing & Delivery

﹀

Post-production is where the final look is polished. You receive all of the files from the shoot, mark the photos you want edited, and I deliver a gallery of professionally edited images formatted for your specific needs.

Info &
Policies

How is my date reserved? +

Your date is reserved after I approve the session details and the $30 booking deposit is paid. The remaining balance is due according to the terms provided with your booking.

What happens if the weather is bad? +

For outdoor sessions, we can reschedule when weather would prevent us from creating the planned work. We’ll make that decision as the session approaches.

Can I bring multiple outfits? +

Yes. Outfit changes can be planned during the consultation so they support the concept and fit comfortably within the session.

How do I choose my final photos? +

You’ll receive a proof gallery of all the photos after the shoot and select the images you want professionally edited. I will pick out my favorites as well.

Do you travel? +

Yes. Sessions more than 2 hours from the Lowell area may include an additional travel fee depending on distance.
