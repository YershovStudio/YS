01

Consultation

We begin with a discussion to define your project goals and create a Pinterest board to visualize ideas. We discuss the aesthetic direction, outfit ideas, and location to create the best possible results.



02

Production & Creation

On the shoot day, we execute the plan in a studio or on-location. This phase is entirely focused on visual storytelling, using lighting, poses and the environment to capture imagery that tells your unique story.



03

Editing & Delivery

Post-production is where the final look is polished. You receive all of the files from the shoot, mark the photos you want edited, and I deliver a gallery of professionally edited images formatted for your specific needs.
